# AgeLens V2 — ARISE 2026 Alignment

## Working Project Positioning

**From reproducible biological-age replication to transportable external validation: the AgeLens framework for contemporary NHANES.**

## Strongest ARISE Topic Categories

Primary:

- Biological age estimation and epigenetic modeling
- Survival and longitudinal modeling

Potential secondary categories after V2 completion:

- Machine learning for outcome prediction
- AI applications in health and biomedical research
- Development or application of R packages or software

The machine-learning and AI categories must be selected only if V2 completes a governed, validated explainable extension.

## Aging Application

AgeLens addresses a core aging-research problem: whether a biomarker-derived age measure can be transported across laboratory eras and remain informative for survival, morbidity, and functional health in a nationally representative population.

V1 established reproducible score construction and all-cause mortality validation. V2 has frozen serious difficulty walking or climbing stairs as its primary non-mortality outcome and will test functional-health relevance, transportability, and incremental utility while preserving complex-survey inference and transparent governance.

## Minimum ARISE-Ready V2 Package

1. the frozen `DLQ050` mobility-disability outcome;
2. one conventional adjusted external-validation result;
3. one incremental-performance comparison;
4. a prespecified transportability analysis;
5. one clear figure summarizing the V1-to-V2 scientific progression;
6. reproducibility and validation checks;
7. a concise limitations statement;
8. a 5–10 minute English presentation.

## Scope Protection

The ARISE deadline shall not justify:

- changing the primary outcome after seeing results;
- adding unsupported subgroups;
- selecting favorable metrics post hoc;
- labeling conventional regression as AI;
- hiding null or negative findings;
- weakening V1 or V2 validation gates.
